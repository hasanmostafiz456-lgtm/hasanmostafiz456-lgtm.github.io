# Research

## Undergraduate Thesis / Biotech Project

### Isolation and Characterization of Heavy Metal (Lead)-Resistant Bacteria for Bioremediation of Industrial Wastewater

**Course:** BTE450: Biotech Project  
**Institution:** BRAC University  
**Year:** 2025  
**Contributors:** Nazif Muhammad, Hasan Mostafiz Tabib, Prome Saha

This project investigated lead-resistant bacteria isolated from contaminated industrial environments for potential application in wastewater bioremediation.

The study incorporated atomic absorption spectroscopy, metagenomic analysis, bacterial isolation, 16S rDNA sequencing, scanning electron microscopy, and assessment of lead-removal efficiency.
