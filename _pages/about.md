I am a Master of Science in Biotechnology student at BRAC University with research interests in animal biotechnology, microbial genomics, microbiome research, molecular microbiology, bioinformatics, and host–microbe interactions.

My academic interests span both computational and experimental biotechnology, with particular interest in animal models, biomedical research, microbial genomics, sequencing technologies, infectious disease research, and translational applications.

## Current Research

### Diversity-Generating Retroelements in the Human Gut Microbiome

I am currently involved in computational research investigating Diversity-Generating Retroelements (DGRs) in human gut microbiome sequence datasets, with an interest in microbial sequence diversity, adaptation, and evolution.

## Previous Research

### Lead-Resistant Bacteria for Wastewater Bioremediation

My undergraduate thesis investigated lead-resistant bacteria and their potential application in wastewater bioremediation.
