# Conference Contributions

## The Evolutionary Landscape of Diversity-Generating Retroelements (DGR) in South Asian Gut Commensals

**Presentation ID:** PP012  
**Authors:** Hasan Mostafiz Tabib, Adiba Tasnim Khan Shazin, Tushar Ahmed Shishi  
**Affiliation:** Department of Biotechnology, School of Life Sciences, BRAC University  
**Type:** Conference Poster Abstract  
**Year:** 2026

This work examines Diversity-Generating Retroelements (DGRs) in South Asian gut commensals, with emphasis on hypervariation, target-gene diversification, and the potential role of bacteriophages in microbial adaptation.
